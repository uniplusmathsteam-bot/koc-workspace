Distance and slope

Δx means the change in x. Δy means the change in y.

\(\Delta x=x_2-x_1\qquad \Delta y=y_2-y_1\)

After this, the long difference is just Δx or Δy.

Where each coordinate goes

\(A(x_1,y_1)\) \(B(x_2,y_2)\)
\(x_1\) \(x_2\)
\(y_1\) \(y_2\)

\(AB=\sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2+(y_2-y_1)^2}\)

\(\Delta x=x_2-x_1,\ \Delta y=y_2-y_1,\ \text{so } AB=\sqrt{\Delta x^2+\Delta y^2}\)

\(x_1=2,\ y_1=11,\ x_2=-4,\ y_2=3\)

\(\sqrt{(-4-2)^2+(3-11)^2}=\sqrt{(-6)^2+(-8)^2}\)

\(=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}\)

\(AB=10\)

Slope uses the same two changes

\(m=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\)

Same sign: the line climbs, so m > 0. Opposite signs: the line falls, so m < 0.

Zero, and undefined

A horizontal line has slope 0. A vertical line has undefined slope, because Δx is 0 and you cannot divide.

On a polygon, sort the sides by looking: uphill, downhill, flat, or straight up and down.

Same slope means collinear

\(m_{AB}=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=\dfrac{2-(-7)}{3-10}=\dfrac{9}{-7}\)

\(m_{BC}=\dfrac{11-2}{-4-3}=\dfrac{9}{-7}\)

same slope, so A, B and C lie on this line

Intercepts and a point with a slope

Between \((-8,0)\) and \((0,2)\), Δy = 2 and Δx = 8.

\(m=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}=\dfrac{2}{8}=\dfrac{1}{4}\)

The other way: a point and a slope already fix the line. The slope says how y changes when x changes.

Through \((6,2)\) with \(m=-\dfrac{2}{3}\): \(\;y-2=\left(-\dfrac{2}{3}\right)(x-6)\)

\(x=0\) gives \((0,6)\). \(y=0\) gives \((9,0)\).

A point on the y-axis is \((0,q)\). Put it into the same equation and solve for \(q\).

Inclination is the same ratio

\(m=\tan\theta=\dfrac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}\)

\(\theta\) is the angle with the positive x-axis. Among uphill lines, steeper means a larger slope. Downhill lines stay negative.
