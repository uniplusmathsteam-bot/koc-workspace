Structure Breach

A chemistry-based zombie shooter. Each zombie is labeled with a substance from your notes. Identify which of the four structures it has, switch to the counter weapon, and clear four escalating waves — including the Polymorph boss.

Goal

  • Read the substance on each zombie (e.g. NaCl, diamond, CO₂) and decide its structure.
  • Only the correct counter weapon deals damage. Wrong weapons deal zero damage and flash “WRONG!”.
  • All four structure types can appear in every wave.
  • Clear wave 4 by defeating Polymorph, who changes substance / structure over time.

Structure counters (from the notes)

Giant metallic
Examples: Na, K, Li, Mg, Ca, Fe, Cu, Zn, Hg…
Counter: Shock Pin (1)
Giant ionic
Examples: NaCl, CsCl, Al₂O₃, MgCl₂, NaBr…
Counter: Melt / Solvent (2)
Giant covalent
Examples: graphite, diamond, SiO₂, Si, B
Counter: Lattice Breaker (3)
Simple molecular
Examples: CO₂, I₂, CH₄, Cl₂, Br₂, NH₃, H₂O…
Counter: Scatter Blast (4)

Sector: Breach Lab

  • One combined arena — mixed substances from all four structures spawn together.
  • Use weapon 2 against ionic compounds (NaCl, etc.).
  • Eight solid obstacles spawn in random spots each breach — they block shots and movement; enemies will path around them to chase you.
  • Waves 1–4 get harder (more enemies, higher HP/speed). Wave 4 includes Polymorph.

Boss: Polymorph

  • Appears on wave 4 with a rotating square frame.
  • Its substance label changes as it shifts structure — identify the new substance and switch weapons.
  • When the label is an ionic compound (e.g. NaCl), switch to weapon 2.
Tips: Trust the substance label. Wrong weapon = no damage + red “WRONG!” popup. Press Esc or on-screen PAUSE to pause.

Paused

Press Esc, on-screen PAUSE, or Resume to continue.
