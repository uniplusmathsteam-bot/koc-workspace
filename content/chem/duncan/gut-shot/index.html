3-Minute Gut Run

Objective: get the most points in 3 minutes. Match the enzyme to the food. Shots spend ammo — select 1–4, then press R (or Refill) to refill.

  • Yellow carbs → Amylase · Red protein → Protease · Orange fat → Lipase (bile splits big fats)
  • Don’t shoot glucose. Fibre fades on its own.
  • 1–4 or tap an enzyme, then R / Refill fills that ammo to 20 (takes 2s). Cap is 20. Other ammos stay usable while one is refilling.
  • Phone: left stick move · right stick aim & hold to fire · Pause button
  • Esc or Pause. Timer pauses with the game.
