Controls

  • Keys stay as Q · W · E · R — tools swap between those slots after every player attack
  • Each phase has its own Calcarius health bar — empty it to advance
  • The boss attack timer does not reset when you attack
  • Wrong tool: you take a little damage, and may heal the boss a bit
  • Excess needed: In Phase 3 press Water twice, and in Phase 4 press Carbon dioxide twice, to deal one hit
  • Warning — Mist phase: a wrong tool can make Calcarius snap all the way back to hard rock. Use excess Carbon dioxide carefully!
