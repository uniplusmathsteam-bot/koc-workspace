    Nature of the test

    The flame test is a physical test. Heating does not change the chemical identity of the metal cation; electrons are excited and then emit characteristic light as they return to lower energy levels. The observed flame colour is therefore a physical property used for qualitative identification.

    All four flame colours in this lab

    Potassium ion Lilac
    Sodium ion Golden yellow
    Calcium ion Brick red
    Copper ion Bluish green

    Correct procedures

    Procedures of cleaning the platinum / nichrome wire:

    1. Dip the platinum wire / nichrome wire into concentrated hydrochloric acid.
    2. Heat the end of the wire strongly in a non-luminous flame.
    3. Repeat the above steps until no characteristic flame color can be observed

    Procedures of flame test:

    1. Dip a clean platinum wire / nichrome wire into concentrated hydrochloric acid.
    2. Dip the wire into the sample.
    3. Heat the end of the wire strongly in a non-luminous flame. Observe the flame color.

    Note: A nichrome wire also works as a replacement of a platinum wire for the flame test. After heating, always allow the wire to cool before dipping it back into concentrated hydrochloric acid — a red-hot wire will cause the acid to boil away violently.
