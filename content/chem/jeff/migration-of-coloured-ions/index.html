Key idea

In an electric field, coloured ions migrate: anions toward the anode (+), cations toward the cathode (−). The blurry streak is the trail of the moving coloured spot on the moist paper.

Notes

  1. Note 1: If the paper is not connected to d.c. supply, the purple color patch will diffuse to all directions.
  2. Note 2: To speed up the experiment, higher voltage can be applied.

Key idea
